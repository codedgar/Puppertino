A date picker lets people choose a date, a time, or both. The graphical style shows a month calendar; the compact style is a text field with a stepper.

## Preview

<div style="margin: 16px 0 20px; padding: 32px 24px; background: var(--p-bg-secondary); border-radius: 10px; display: flex; gap: 40px; align-items: flex-start; flex-wrap: wrap;">
  <div class="p-calendar" aria-label="Choose date"></div>
</div>

The calendar is generated by `datepickers.js`. Click a day to select it, and use the arrows or the dot (today) to navigate.

## Anatomy: calendar

The card is 138.5px wide and 148px tall with a 1px hairline ring at 9.8% black and a white background — the native fitting size. The header holds the month title, 15px bold at a 2.5px inset, with previous, today, and next controls in secondary gray. The weekday row runs Monday first, 11px at weight 590 secondary, closed by a 1px separator. Day cells sit on an 18px row pitch with 16px pills and 11px numbers; days outside the month drop to tertiary.

Today (when unselected) shows a `#DCDCDC` rounded cell with the number in the accent color — the unfocused-control look of the real picker. The selected day fills with `rgb(0, 100, 225)` and turns its number white.

| Class | Role |
|---|---|
| `.p-calendar` | The card. `datepickers.js` builds header, weekdays, and grid. |
| `data-p-date` | Initial selected date (`2026-01-09`). Defaults to today's month, nothing selected. |
| `.p-calendar-day` | Generated day button. `.p-today`, `.p-selected`, `.p-outside` states. |

Selection dispatches a bubbling `p-change` event with `detail.date`.

## Analog clock (experimental)

An analog clock companion (`.p-clock`) ships in `pickers.css` and `datepickers.js` but is experimental: the rendering is not consistent across browsers, it has no preview here, and its geometry may change between releases. The targets are 122px overall, a 5px metallic bezel, 13px numbers on a 44px ring, black hour and minute hands, and a thin red second hand. Hands rotate via `--p-clock-h`, `--p-clock-m`, and `--p-clock-s`; set `data-p-clock-time="9:41:00"` for a static time or `data-p-clock-live` to run. Avoid it in production.

## Compact style

The field-and-stepper picker is a composition of existing components, matching the system size ladder (28px regular with 13px text, 22px small with 11px, 18px mini with 9px):

```html
<span style="display: inline-flex; gap: 4px; align-items: center;">
  <input class="p-field" style="width: 132px;" value="1/9/2026, 9:41 AM" aria-label="Date and time" />
  <!-- .p-stepper from forms -->
</span>
```

## Dark mode

All colors come from the system's resolved dynamic colors: the card becomes `#1E1E1E`, labels `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.847)`, the hairline 9.8% white, today's cell 14% white, and the selection deepens to `rgb(0, 89, 209)`. Both `.p-dark-mode` and `.p-auto-dark-mode` are supported.

## Accessibility

Day cells are real buttons in a `role="grid"` container and take the standard focus ring. The selected day carries `aria-pressed="true"`.
